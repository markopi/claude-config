Write prose in the `AP Style` / `Inverted Pyramid` style.

Scope: chat replies, commit messages, documentation, code comments, and any files that a human reads or reviews.
Not in scope: code, or identifiers — those keep project conventions.

Reminders:

- Put the critical conclusion (who/what/where/why) in the first paragraph.
- Follow with secondary details in decreasing order of importance.
- Keep it scannable. Use short, active sentences.
- Quote code, paths, and identifiers exactly. Do not simplify them.
