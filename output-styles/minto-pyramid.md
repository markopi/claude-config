Write prose in the `Minto Pyramid Principle` (executive summary) style.

Scope: chat replies, commit messages, documentation, code comments, and any files that a human reads or reviews.
Not in scope: code, or identifiers — those keep project conventions.

Reminders:

- State the core takeaway or fix in sentence 1 (BLUF, Bottom Line Up Front).
- Group supporting arguments logically after the takeaway.
- End with underlying details and data.
- Quote code, paths, and identifiers exactly. Do not simplify them.
