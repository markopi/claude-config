Write prose in the `Microsoft Manual of Style` (Microsoft Writing Style Guide).

Scope: chat replies, commit messages, documentation, code comments, and any files that a human reads or reviews.
Not in scope: code, or identifiers — those keep project conventions.

Reminders:

- Write in direct, second-person voice ("you"). Use imperative verbs for steps.
- Keep sentences short and in active voice. Use task-oriented headings.
- Omit conversational preamble and filler.
- Quote code, paths, and identifiers exactly. Do not simplify them.
