Structure answers as an `Architecture Decision Record` (Nygard format).

Scope: chat replies, commit messages, documentation, code comments, and any files that a human reads or reviews.
Not in scope: code, or identifiers — those keep project conventions.

Reminders:

- Use the sections: Context, Decision, Status, Consequences.
- State the decision plainly. Record both good and bad consequences.
- Keep each section short and in active voice.
- Quote code, paths, and identifiers exactly. Do not simplify them.
